## Decisions
- D-001 | Stack: SQLite (Python stdlib) + pandas/scipy + Streamlit. No Power BI, no PostgreSQL, no ML. Reason: side-target project, minimal owner effort. (Amended by D-015: DuckDB was the original plan.)
- D-002 | Delivery KPIs use `order_status = 'delivered'` AND non-null delivered date (excludes 8 delivered orders with no delivery date).
- D-003 | One review per order: keep the latest by `review_answer_timestamp` (tie-break `review_creation_date`, then review_id); 551 rows dropped.
- D-004 | "Seller dispatch time" = carrier handoff - purchase timestamp (avoids negative approval-to-carrier gaps in 1,359 orders). Stage metrics (dispatch/transit) use only orders where purchase <= carrier <= delivered ("stage-valid").
- D-005 | `shipping_limit_date` values >= 2019-01-01 (4 items; max 2020-04-09) are outliers, excluded from dispatch-compliance KPIs.
- D-006 | Metric naming: "observed-selling rate", not "activation rate", because the order data is a sample.
- D-007 | Funnel downstream metrics use only deals with >= 90 days of order data after won_date (667 deals).
- D-008 | Acquired vs other sellers: acquired = deal sellers observed in order data; comparison group = "non-funnel" sellers (may include sellers from leads outside the closed-deal set or sellers who joined independently).
- D-009 | GMV proxy = sum of item price at distinct-order grain; freight reported separately; payments table only for reconciliation (99.61% match).
- D-010 | Raw folders keep their names with spaces (`EcommercePub dataset`, `Marketing funnel`); scripts locate files recursively by file name.
- D-011 | Analysis window: orders purchased 2017-01-01 to 2018-08-31 (2016 is sparse; 2018-09/10 are truncated).
- D-012 | Lateness is date-level: delivered date vs estimated date compared as calendar dates, because estimated dates carry no time of day. Timestamp-level comparison would also flag same-day deliveries as late (8.13% vs 6.79% in the window). Report both only in the robustness section.
- D-013 | Effective data end for observation windows: 2018-08-31.
- D-014 | Geolocation file is not loaded; no dim_geo; no map analysis (state-level only). It is still to be profiled by audit.py (L-007).
- D-015 | SQLite replaces DuckDB (DuckDB could not be installed in the build environment; SQLite needs no install and supports the CTE/window-function SQL used). The app reads CSV files with pandas; no parquet or DuckDB at runtime.
- D-016 | Minimum-n rules: origin / landing page >= 100 leads; SDR >= 10 deals; downstream-by-origin chi-square >= 30 deals with window; seller group comparison >= 10 orders per seller; seller Pareto >= 20 orders; simulator-eligible sellers >= 30 orders; lanes >= 50 orders; categories >= 100 orders.
- D-017 | "Bad months" for the shift-share = months with late rate > 10% (2017-11, 2018-02, 2018-03 in the earlier run). The threshold is a round number chosen AFTER viewing the monthly series; sensitivity (8% / 12%) must be checked in the robustness package.
- D-018 | Seller-level KPIs use single-seller delivered orders only; the 1,274 multi-seller orders stay in order-level KPIs.
- D-019 | Order primary category = category of item 1 of the order.
- D-020 | Dispatch compliance = carrier handoff date <= earliest non-outlier shipping limit date of the order; single-seller orders only; date-level.
- D-021 | "Review created before delivery" flag is date-level (review_creation_date has no time of day).
- D-022 | Quarterly cohort toggle dropped; monthly retention heatmap only (keeps scope lean).
- D-023 | Adjusted odds ratio = Mantel-Haenszel over strata review_pre_delivery x customer_state, outcome 1-star, reviewed delivered-valid orders in the window; strata with no late or no on-time reviewed orders add nothing. Reported with a bootstrap CI (WP1). Replaces (a) the WP0 implementation, which used 3 strata by delay band and returned 16.42, i.e. no adjustment at all (it sits on the crude 16.46), and (b) the unrecoverable earlier 25-strata version (4.38, retired). WP0 quoted 4.23 and 4.16 from throw-away commands, not saved code: not citable until the saved pipeline produces them.
- D-024 | Simulator definition. Target = median of seller-average dispatch days among eligible sellers (>= 30 stage-valid single-seller orders; 611 sellers; 2.561 days). "Worst X%" = top round(611 x X) sellers by late-order COUNT, so large sellers rank first (label it that way in the app). The 1-star effect is always a range: lower = flipped orders x post-delivery gap (18.72% - 6.58% = 12.14 pp), upper = flipped orders x crude gap (53.76% - 6.60% = 47.16 pp), because about three quarters of late orders' reviews predate delivery (L-009). The crude-only figure (256 at 10%) must never be quoted alone; at 10% the range is about 66 to 256 (arithmetic from the 542 flipped orders; the code must regenerate it). The lost run's 575 / 199 are retired.
- D-025 | Seller activity for cohort retention = any delivered-valid order containing an item sold by the seller (multi-seller orders count). D-018 (single-seller orders only) stays for quality KPIs (on-time, dispatch, review), which cannot be attributed to one seller on a multi-seller order. WP0b changes sql/05 accordingly and reports both definitions side by side. Whether the change reproduces the earlier 132/197 vs 262/533 is a diagnostic, not the reason for the change.
- D-026 | First sale within 90 days keeps the pipeline definition (timestamp level, inclusive: first delivered order on or after won_ts and within 90 days). The pipeline gives 288; the lost run's 290 is unreproducible. WP0 explained the gap as strict < 90 vs <= 90, which is wrong: the SQL is already inclusive. WP1 reports a date-level variant as a sensitivity.
- D-027 | Mann-Whitney effect size r = 2U/(n_late x n_ontime) - 1, negative when late orders have lower scores (about -0.64). P-values below 1e-300 print as "<1e-300". The WP0 code printed +0.638 under an inverted docstring and p = 0.0.
- D-028 | Repo hygiene: the pipeline writes app_orders.csv.gz and app_funnel.csv only (WP0 also exported duplicate out_app_orders.csv, 17 MB, and out_app_funnel.csv). outputs/audit/ is small and is committed as provenance (removed from .gitignore). The two local commits are unpushed, so history may be rewritten once, before the first push.
- D-029 | Test policy: structural tests (grain, GMV conservation, late flag, stage times with tolerance 1e-3 days, funnel waterfall, window) run against the real database when it exists and against the synthetic fixture otherwise. The funnel test recomputes from the raw CSVs with pandas, independent of the SQL, not against hard-coded constants. Every report states how many tests ran against real data. (Mechanics revised by D-030.)
- D-030 | Test suite v2 (2026-10-14; keeps the intent of D-029, replaces its mechanics). Why: the v1 suite skipped 10 of 13 tests on any machine without the real database (the synthetic fallback never ran because its fixture depended on the real-database fixture), and its synthetic fixture was 4 orders, so passing proved little. Now: (1) tests/synth_db.py holds a 13-order hand-built dataset with known answers (window edges, delivered without date, multi-seller order, stage-invalid order, date-level vs timestamp-level lateness, review dedup, deal sellers) and the SQL is checked against it on every machine; (2) structural tests (grain, GMV conservation, late flag, stage times 1e-3 days, window both directions, funnel shape, cohort coherence) are parametrised over [synthetic] and, when the pipeline database exists, [real]; (3) the funnel is recomputed in pandas from the raw tables and compared with the SQL, on synthetic and real data; (4) real-only checks carry the marker `real`, skip with a stated reason and never fail for a missing file (row counts, simulator on real orders, saved adjusted OR and Mann-Whitney sign, saved outputs consistent with the database); (5) paths are anchored to the project root (env overrides OLIST_RAW, OLIST_DB, OLIST_OUT), pytest.ini sets testpaths and -ra; (6) the pytest header says which mode ran and the footer says how many passed tests ran on real data. Expected (30 tests, 38 with the real database): nothing present 26 passed + 4 skipped; clean clone with the committed outputs/ folder 27 passed + 3 skipped; machine with the real database and outputs 38 passed, 0 skipped (12 of them on real data). Rule for agents: never skip, loosen or delete a test to get green; paste the full pytest output and fix the cause.
- D-031 | Simulator 1-star range is ordered by construction: est_1star_low = min and est_1star_high = max of (flipped orders x post-delivery gap) and (flipped orders x crude gap). Before, low <= high held only because the crude gap exceeds the post-delivery gap in the real data; the v1 test asserted it on random data, where it can fail. Real-data values are unchanged (5%: 44 to 169; 10%: 66 to 256; 20%: 93 to 360). | src/simulator.py, tests test_d1/test_d2/test_e2

## Limitations
- L-001 | Order data is a sample: absent from orders means "not observed", not "never sold".
- L-002 | 462 deal sellers cannot be analysed downstream; downstream funnel metrics rest on 376-380 sellers.
- L-003 | Lost vs still-open leads are indistinguishable (no outcome flag for non-closed leads).
- L-004 | No cost, margin, CAC or SDR-cost data; no costed recommendation is possible.
- L-005 | Four closed_deals columns are >90% null and unusable.
- L-006 | Reviews are observational; some precede delivery or relate to non-delivered orders; causal claims are not allowed.
- L-007 | CLOSED 2026-10-11: geolocation profiled (V-009).
- L-008 | business_segment, lead_type and SDR exist only for closed deals, so they cannot explain lead->deal conversion; they support only downstream/onboarding views.
- L-009 | Survey timing: about three quarters of late orders' reviews were written before the delivery date (earlier run: 74.5%), so the crude late-vs-on-time review gap overstates the effect of delay on post-delivery satisfaction. Report crude and timing-split figures together.
- L-010 | Acquired-vs-non-funnel comparison is observational with selection effects (acquired sellers are observed only if they appear in orders; cohorts are defined by first observed sale).
- L-011 | Origin-level and paid-vs-organic tests are exploratory; no multiple-comparison correction.
- L-012 | CLOSED 2026-10-11: Mantel-Haenszel adjusted OR now computed with 95% bootstrap CI [3.70, 4.66] across review_pre_delivery x state strata (D-023).

## Hypothesis status (all headline hypotheses now tested and verified on real data)
- H1 delay -> low reviews: SUPPORTED as an association. Crude 1-star 53.8% (late) vs 6.6% (on time); effect shrinks to +12.1 pp when only post-delivery reviews are used; Mantel-Haenszel adjusted OR 4.16 (95% CI 3.70 to 4.66, 47 strata) vs crude 16.46. WP0b verified from saved code.
- H2 "62% of delay is seller dispatch": NOT SUPPORTED. Dispatch accounts for about 13% of the extra time of late orders (carrier transit about 87%); by larger excess stage: 16.6% dispatch vs 83.4% transit. Verified on real data: dispatch 13.25% (+3.04 days), carrier transit 86.75% (+19.91 days).
- H3 origin/landing page associated with conversion: SUPPORTED for lead->deal (Cramer's V 0.13 origin, 0.19 landing page). Downstream (90-day first sale) by origin: chi2 9.67 (4 dof, p = 0.046, V 0.13). Paid vs organic downstream difference: +15.85 pp (95% CI 5.41 to 26.29 pp, exploratory, L-011). WP0b verified from saved code.
- H4 acquired sellers perform like others: REFUTED in this data (acquired look better on on-time rate, dispatch, reviews, month-2/3 retention), with selection caveat L-010. Month 2 retention under D-025: acquired 67.0% vs non-funnel 49.2% (+17.85 pp, CI 10.03 to 25.67 pp). 2018 sellers: on-time 97.3% vs 93.2% (r = +0.31), dispatch 1.88 vs 2.46 days (r = -0.31), review 4.42 vs 4.21 (r = +0.29). WP0b verified from saved code.
